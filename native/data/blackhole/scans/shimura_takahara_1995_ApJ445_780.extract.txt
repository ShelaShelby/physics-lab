#--- begin p. 780, abstract
Radiation spectrum and the spectral hardening factor of an accretion disk around a Schwarzschild black
hole with a mass of 1.4, 3, and 10 M_sun are determined by solving the vertical structure and radiative transfer
self-consistently. When a viscous parameter alpha ~ 0.1, for accretion rates close to the Eddington limit, the local
spectrum can be described by the diluted blackbody spectrum with the spectral hardening factor f ~ 1.8-2.0
which is almost independent of a radial position and black hole mass. For ~10% of the Eddington rate, the
local spectrum emitted from an inner region can be approximated by the diluted blackbody with f ~ 1.7. For
accretion rates lower than 1% of the Eddington limit, the local spectrum cannot be fitted with any f.
