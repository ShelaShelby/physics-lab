p. 534, abstract
Returning radiation is radiation emitted by an accretion disk which returns to its surface due to
gravitational focusing or the shape of the disk. While it is unimportant for thin disks around non-
rotating black holes, returning radiation will have significant effects on the structure and appear-
ance of thin disks around rotating holes with a/M ~ 1. We calculate the flux of the returning
radiation as a function of radius for a/M = 0.9, 0.99, 0.9981, and 0.9999, treating the propaga-
tion of the radiation by general-relativistic geometric optics and treating the disk as thin.
