#--- begin p. 233, the observed bolometric flux
As explained by Ellis (1971), the observed bolometric flux F0
will differ from the intrinsic flux of the source FS by the inverse 4th
power of the redshift factor 1+z. The 4th power may be under-
stood as arising from (i) the loss of energy suffered by each photon
due to the redshift (one power), (ii) the lower measured rate of
arrival of photons due to the time dilatation (one power), and
(iii) the relativistic correction to solid-angle measurements (two
powers).
#--- end
