The habitable zone of the Sun (0.49.4, Orbit Lab: the green band and the Sun's card).

kopparapu_2014_arxiv1404.5292v2.pdf
    Saved as received on 11 Nov 2026 from https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.5292 (v2, 12 May 2014):
    R. K. Kopparapu, R. M. Ramirez, J. SchottelKotte, J. F. Kasting, S. Domagal-Goldman, V. Eymet,
    "Habitable zones around main-sequence stars: dependence on planetary mass", ApJL 787, L29 (2014).
    Used: Table 1, row "Seff_sun (1 M_Earth)": runaway greenhouse 1.107 (the inner edge), maximum greenhouse 0.356 (the outer edge),
    and Eq. (5), d = (L / L_sun / Seff)^0.5 AU. For the Sun (L = L_sun): 0.9504 AU and 1.6760 AU.
    Until 0.49.3 the lab drew 0.95 - 1.67 AU with "no named source" (the outer edge cut, not rounded, from 1.676).
    The paper says its 1D model puts Earth right on the inner limit (its section 3); the band is this conservative estimate, not a fact about where life can be.
