The two-photon decay of the metastable 2s level (0.46.1: the Atom Lab's "2s has no one-photon jump down" message and the 2s lifetime note).

jentschura_hep-ph_0409271v2.pdf
    Saved as received on 8 Nov 2026 from https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0409271 :
    U. D. Jentschura, "Two-Loop Bethe Logarithms for Higher Excited S Levels", arXiv:hep-ph/0409271v2 (23 Sep 2004).
    Eq. (11) and the text after it: the 2S two-photon width of hydrogen-like systems is Gamma = 8.229 Z^6 s^-1 (citing Shapiro and Breit, Phys. Rev. 113, 179 (1959), and Klarsfeld, Phys. Lett. A 30, 382 (1969)),
    "The lifetime of a hydrogenic 2S level is thus 0.1215 Z^-6 s." The Z^6 law is the non-relativistic scaling; relativity changes it for heavy ions.
