#--- begin Equation (2)
The Dirac point-nucleus binding energy $E_{\rm D}$ is given by
\begin{align} \label{eq2}
E_{\rm D} = mc^2\, \left[
\sqrt{1 - \frac{(\Za)^2}{N^2}} - 1
\right]\,,
\end{align}
where
\begin{align} \label{eq:N}
N &\ = \sqrt{(n_r+\gamma)^2 + (\Za)^2}\,,\\
\gamma &\ = \sqrt{\kappa^2-(\Za)^2}\,,
\end{align}
$n_r = n - |\kappa|$ is the radial quantum number, $n$ is the principal quantum number, and
$\kappa$ is the angular momentum-parity quantum number.
