## What changed (New UI On, both labs)
- **Panel handle (A1):** the toolbar's Controls button is gone. A tab (24 x 56 u) with an arrow rides on the panel's left edge, centred on its height: arrow left = open (closed: on the window's right edge), arrow right = close. Click toggles; the ✕ in the panel header still closes. It is put in front of every window. There was **no keyboard shortcut** for the panel before (only the toolbar button), so none to keep; I did not add one (new feature).
- **Slide:** 180 ms, smoothstep ease (`app/shell.*`: `slideStep`, `slideEase`, `slidRect`, `handleRect`, `dockRect`, `kSlideSeconds`). At once when Settings → Motion is reduced (`look().reduced`). While it moves or is open the layout is the open one (toolbar and readout left of it), so nothing slides under it. Per-frame CPU work outside the slide: one clamp-and-compare (`slideStep` returns at once); during the slide the panel is drawn at its moving place. Cost not measured (nothing to measure in software rendering; see "Not tested").
- **Glow / outline (A2), root cause:** every new-UI panel went through `ui::panelDecor(Floating)` → `shadowBehind`, which in Aero draws an 18 pt shadow offset 6 pt down plus a 14 pt outer glow, even round the small Legend pill; the plain themes had a 16 u drop shadow. Fix in the one shared place: `panelDecor(kind, hug)` (hug = new UI only) draws shadow and glow as a 2 px soft edge (`kHaloMaxPx`), no offset, no mirrored shine, and returns how far it reached; the plain themes' shadow is a 2 px soft edge too. All new-UI hover paint goes through one helper (`hoverFill`) that paints inside the widget's own rounded rectangle and logs it. (The widgets' hover was already inside; the spill was the panels.)
- Found on the way: Dear ImGui's 32 px minimum window size made every new-UI window under 32 px bigger than its place (the 24 u handle; the Legend pill was 32 high, not its 30 u). `beginPanel` now allows small windows.
- **Tidy-up (A3):** (a) the "stray glyph" at the right of the Time speed header was the picture's **Eris** label showing through the 0.95-opaque panel (zoomed crop); the Controls panel is now solid in the plain themes (Aero's glass was already opaque). (b) The Orbit Lab has had no Old panel button since 5c (all its controls moved); the Atom Lab's stays in the top bar: unchanged, recorded in DECISIONS. (c) Section headers: words on the content's left edge, one arrow size ending on the content's right edge, hover running 8 u into the padding. (d) Rows ("Notes about this choice", Pick list) also start on the content's left edge in the panel; check boxes, segmented buttons and buttons already used the content's edges. Sliders keep their 2 px + half-knob inset (the knob must fit). (e) Every section starts 4 u + rule below the previous item (`Panel::gap` records what the last item left). (f) Panel Caption text (13 px) is drawn at Body size (15 px, floor 14) inside the Controls panel only (`sh::bodyFloor`); no new size.
