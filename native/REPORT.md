## What is built (Settings → New UI (preview), last section, Off by default; Off = today's app)
- **Type scale** `app/shell.*`: Caption 13 / Body 15 / Title 17 / Key 28 units, floors 12 / 14 / 15 / 22 px, × Windows scale × Text size (`PL_SCALE` stands in for the scale in tests). Controls 30 u, top bar 44, spacing 4–24.
- **Frame:** the picture fills the window. Top bar (menu, breadcrumbs with a Labs drop-down, centre chip with Today, search, icons: other lab, Learn, Achievements, 💡 card, Theme, Settings, Old panel). Toolbar bottom centre (icon button, button with caret, separator, 2D/3D segmented + caret, "…" overflow). Readout bottom right. Legend (collapsed) and subject card that collapse to their title (state saved per lab). Dropdowns under the chip, panels above the toolbar, one at a time. The centre shift moves the selected body out from under open panels (eased 0.3 s). Esc, Ctrl+K (search), Space. Aero / Y2K use their own panel style; the top bar uses the stage 2 page cache.
- **Reading view** (`ui/reading.*`): About, History, changelogs and guides in a centred 34 em column over the 60 % dimmed picture, H1/H2/H3, contents (rail when the window is wider than 60 em + column, else a menu), ✕ and Esc.

## Found
- **Bug found by the test:** Text size Extra large gave Body 19 px, not 20 (1.3 as a float is 1.29999995, 15 × it rounds down); fixed (doubles).
- **Bug found by the test:** the panel shadow was drawn under the glass, so High contrast panels were 0.75 instead of 0.70 opaque; now outside only.
- **Existing data, not changed in the old layout:** Dark and Black put white (onacc) on the accent #6c9cff = 2.68:1 on chosen buttons; the new UI uses the page colour there (`onAccent`). Source: WCAG 2.x formula, `theme_contrast.py`.
