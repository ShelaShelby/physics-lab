## Finding 6: one relativity line, `kRelativityZ = 27` (atom_core.h)
- **Chosen from the size of the correction.** For a point nucleus the Dirac 1s energy mc²(1 − √(1 − (Zα)²)) is above the non-relativistic ½mc²(Zα)² by **0.917 % at Z = 26 and 0.990 % at Z = 27** (about 1 %); at 27 (Zα)² = 0.0388 and the 1s electron moves at Zα = 20 % of c. (Z = 20: 0.54 %, (Zα)² = 0.021.) It is the whole Z nearest to 1 %: the old readout/hint line is kept, the spectrum note moves to it. The percentages are the lab's own Dirac formula; no source was read (About says so).
- **Old → new.** Spectrum note: warned from Z ≥ 20, now from Z ≥ 27 (same words). Readout and hint: unchanged words, now use the constant. **New readout line at every Z** (under ⓘ More): "model: one electron, non-relativistic (no relativity, fine structure, Lamb shift or spin). For a point nucleus the Dirac equation puts the 1s level 0.13 % deeper than this lab's." (Z = 1: 0.0013 %; Z = 118: 33 %; from the formula above.) The heavy-atom line (Z ≥ 27) stays.
- **Nucleus line:** "chance the electron is inside the nucleus …(1 in …)" now ends "· non-relativistic estimate: for heavy atoms relativity makes the real value larger, and this lab does not work it out". No correction factor invented; "larger" is the standard behaviour of s-electron density at a heavy nucleus (not re-sourced here).
- **Saved references moved (words on purpose):** the Iron-56 spectrum note (2 references, metric and imperial: the web's Z ≥ 20 warning dropped); in the 30 web-compared runs the new model line (28 of 30 readouts; the other 2 are Bohr 1913, which has none) and the nucleus suffix (1 line). The test removes the exact new text and compares the rest word for word.
- Test `testRelativityText`: Z = 1, 19, 20, 26, 27, 92, 118: readout, hint and spectrum note agree (warn only from 27); "non-relativistic" is in the readout at every Z; the model line's percentage equals the independent Dirac calculation (NIST α); the nucleus caveat; the 0.917/0.990 % numbers.

## Finding 15: "avg r: sampled"
- **Old → new:** shown from 300 dots on, bare number → shown **only with the full ring of kCap = 12,000 dots** (before: "…"), as "avg r: sampled 0.79 Å (an estimate, usually within 10 %), theory 0.79 Å".
- **Measured spread** at the full ring, 96 seeded labs (12 orbitals incl. complex states × 8 seeds): mean |difference| from the exact a0(3n² − l(l+1))/2Z **3.1 %**, **95 of 96 within 10 %**, worst **17.7 %** (an earlier 16-orbital probe: median 2.1 %, 95th percentile 6.5 %, 99th 8.9 %, worst 13.2 %). The audit's "a few per cent" was too kind, so the text says "usually within 10 %", not a guarantee. Guard in the test **0.1788** = worst + 1 %; the "usually within 10 %" claim is tested as ≥ 95 % of runs.
- Saved references moved: 15 saved readouts have an "avg r: sampled" part: 8 numbers became "…" (fewer than 12,000 dots), 6 keep their number and get the note, 1 was already "…". The test adapts the web text by this one rule.
