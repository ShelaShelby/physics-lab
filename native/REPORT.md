## How branches are shown, and why
Every way is drawn (one path would pretend it is the only one). Rule, stated in the panel: **likeliest way thick and solid; other measured ways ≥ 0.1 % thinner; below 0.1 % faint hairlines; ways with unmeasured odds thin, label ending "?", and NO likeliest**. Colour = kind of mode; labels α, β⁻ … on the likeliest. A split (SF) has no daughter: three short rays if it is likeliest or ≥ 0.1 %, else not drawn.

## Time
Walk is **never real time**. Auto-walk = one step per 1.6 s of screen time (or a button press). Each step states its drawn lifetime, the half-life, a "random draw from the exponential law", the screen time it was shown after and **how many times faster/slower than real time** (U-238 step: about 10¹⁶–10¹⁷×); a standing note says each lifetime is not a fixed schedule.
**No made-up times:** a nuclide is timed only if stage 3 calls it Measured or Approx (then marked "approximate"). Limit (11), Extrapolated (369), Unknown (87) = 467 nuclides: no time, the chain stops there with the reason, a cross on the chart.
**Unmeasured odds:** the lab's table lists equal shares for them (22 timed nuclides have several modes). The walk uses those **only as a marked stand-in**; every step and label says so; no branch is "likeliest". (Alternative, stopping there, is an owner decision.)
**Odds** are normalised to add up to 1 (179 measured entries do not add to 1; worst 8×10⁻⁵; U-238 lists alpha 1 and fission 5.4×10⁻⁷).

Chance of ending at the textbook end: 0.99999944, 0.999999999, 1.0000000, 1.0000000. No disagreement found.
**Findings (not changed, for stage 2):** (1) Pb-206 and Pb-208 are only *observationally stable* in NUBASE2020 (limit > 2.5 / 2.6 Zy, "A ?"); the app says so (71 such nuclides counted = the brief's 71). (3) The old clock took odds in listed order, so a rare way after a certain one (fission of U-238) could never happen. **Moved on purpose:** `decTick` now uses the normalised odds; all 0.11 tests pass unchanged.
