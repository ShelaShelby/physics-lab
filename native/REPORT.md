**Never lost** (orbit_view.cpp)
- `click`: a click on empty space returns at once (before: `sel = {}`, `follow = false`). A click on another thing while following picks it (glide).
- `drag` without turning: when the last drawing held a followed thing (`held_`), it adds to the new `ux, uy` (the user's slide) instead of `ox, oy` and no longer clears `follow`. `holdAt` places the thing at middle + fx + cx (centre shift) + ux, uy, keeps that 24 points inside the free part (insetRight, insetTop, reserveBottom), and is used by the Body / Moon / Bary, Far and Alpha Centauri follows. Reset view and every direct `select` set ux, uy to 0.
- Esc (shell.cpp, after the panels' own Esc chain: only when nothing else took it, no page, no Journey) calls `letGo()`. Picking the followed thing again lets go as before (fly).
**Smooth**
- `pick(s)` (fly and Far-away things; Reduce motion or a Journey: `select`, at once): the next drawing measures where the new thing is on the screen (glideDX/DY, and the view's depth); over 0.8 s (smoothstep) that offset goes to 0 while the thing's place is taken anew each frame, so a moving planet is reached. It ends exactly (`glide_` off at s >= 1). A direct set (select, a changed selection, follow off, a Journey) drops it.
- Zoom: `glideZoom` / `zoomBy` / `cameraTick` (orbit_panel.cpp): the wheel and + / - over 0.15 s, a pick's zoom over 0.8 s, smoothstep, the last step sets the exact value. `setZoom` (Journey, Learn, slider, benchmark) is at once and cancels a glide; Reduce motion, a Journey, the benchmark: at once.
- A released turn (3D) keeps its speed (measured over the drag's last frames, at most 2.5 rad/s), fading e^-7.5 a second, 0 below 0.05 rad/s (from the cap: stops after about 0.5 s, at most 0.33 rad). A press stops it; a turn set by anyone else (Learn, Journey, benchmark scenes, PL_TILT) stops it. Tilt range +-89.5 degrees (Alpha Centauri keeps +-3.1).
- Kept picture: ux, uy are in pictureKey; every glide ends exactly, so the key is unchanged frame to frame at rest (test d).
- Words: Controls › View options caption and the four Learn guides' "Move the view" lines say: right/Shift-drag slides, what you follow stays followed, Esc / Reset view / picking it again lets go.
