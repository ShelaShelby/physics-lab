# 0.56.0: the graphics card draws the spinning hole's bent sky and disc per pixel (closed formulas)
No source read or saved. The formulas are kerr_closed.h / elliptic.h's (0.55.9), ported to GLSL in single precision.
## The shader (render/shaders.h kKerrSkyFS, built from pieces; render/kerr_sky.h makes it, blackhole/sky_card.h checks it)
- Per pixel: the launch (J15 (A.11), (A.12)), GLa's roots and case (the closed shadow test: where traceClosed sends the ray), the far direction or the horizon, four crossings of the disc's plane, then kKerrFS's colour rules. No table. Anti-aliased edge: GLa's discriminant -A/2 - z^2 - B/(4z) (zero on the edge's spherical orbits, linear across it) over its pixel gradient.
- Size: every function called from one place (R_F and R_J in one duplication loop; ellFP in the ray's one loop over its nine integrals; amAGM in one stage of five amplitudes). Own atan/asin/acos (Mesa's asin was off by 1e-4 rad: am erred 1.5e-4).
- SPIR-V (glslang as check_shaders.py, plain / inlined by -Os as a driver does): kKerrSkyFS 101.7 KB / 62.7 KB; the largest known to run on the owner's card kKerrFS 27.3 / 19.5 KB (3.2 x inlined); the dead 0.55.6 compute shader 61.0 / 251.3 KB (a quarter). check_shaders now fails above 3.5 x or a third of the dead one. **Larger than the range asked**; the guard covers it (ROADMAP).

## The resolution rule
At rest every pixel. While moving (the view changed in the last 0.15 s): if the pass's last time on the card for the whole picture (GL_TIMESTAMP pairs, read without waiting) exceeds half the screen's refresh period, it is drawn at scale sqrt(budget / time) of the width, rounded down to eighths, at least 1/4, into smaller layers stretched over the picture (bilinear). smooth: a new sky and disc in 16 of 16 moved frames, also forced slow (PL_BH_SKYSLOW=100: 1/4 while moving), full resolution at rest, the stars caught up.
