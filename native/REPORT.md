**The camera** (new orbit/camera.h, one struct ViewCam for every path; 2D unchanged, the top-down map)
- 3D is one pinhole: kFieldOfViewDeg = 45 (vertical; one constant, in camera.h, included by orbit_view.h, not copied there), focal F = (H / 2) / tan 22.5 deg, the camera at d = F / px from the look-at plane, so the target's plane shows the zoom's span (scale bar "at Earth's distance", its own readout line). Its axis goes through the held place (the principal point); in the look-at plane the map is 0.74.0's, so ox, oy, the slide, the glide and the zoom about the middle keep their meaning. Screen = pp + F (X / Z, -Y / Z).
- Near plane 0.01 d (a thing there is drawn 100 times its size at the target; nearer leaves nothing useful, and 1 / 0.01 keeps the card's float division far from its limits). Behind or nearer: not drawn. Lines are cut at the plane in 3D (clipNear) on both paths: LinePen::to(Q), kLinesCS/kLinesVS (camOf, cutNear, stepSeg); a piece's curve steps come from its cut chord on both (chordNear). Flat polygons (habitable zones, here and in Alpha Centauri) are cut too (clipPolygonNear).
- Balls at True scale: F tan(asin(R / L)) (planets, moons, the Sun, Alpha Centauri's stars); Easy to see: drawn size x d / Z. The closest zoom keeps the camera 1.5 radii from a followed body (zoomCapFor; the panel's zoomTop and the benchmark use it: Earth 5.14, Saturn 4.17 at 1280 x 800).
- The sky through the same lens: the stars, constellation lines (cut to the picture: a great circle is straight through a pinhole) and names, the Milky Way band, the Gaia map per pixel (Renderer2D::skyMapPinhole, kPlanetFS uMode 3: pixel -> direction by the inverse pinhole). The far-away things are directions from the camera (no "flat" any more), drawn before the Sun and the bodies, so a star behind Saturn is hidden by it. Following a far thing looks at it from the zoom's distance (as a probe was). 2D keeps skyScreen's map (also the Black Hole Lab's).
- Done, every old use of D, f and the 0.05 D clamp: base / flat (orbit_view.cpp), LineCam::base (orbit_lines.h, now `using LineCam = ViewCam`), linesCamera and kLinesCS's project (0.05 * uProj.y), beltScreen and kRocksCS (0.05LF * D; behind: off the picture, uBc.w the near plane), the far things' inView and Alpha Centauri's two "dep - cd < 0.9 D", Alpha Centauri's base / flat, the rings (RingFrame zr, wn; kRingsFS's ringAt solves the pinhole's homography), labels and hits (by proj), the kept-picture key (unchanged state). grep finds no "0.05 * D", "0.05LF * D" or "dep - cd" left.
