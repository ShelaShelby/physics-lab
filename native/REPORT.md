# 0.73.1: a clicked moon is followed (box: llvmpipe; the full UI suite was not run; the Windows result: the final chat message)
**Cause**: orbit_view.cpp's follow block took `b->pos` for a Moon selection too: the PLANET stayed in the middle for every moon.
**Fix**
- One function for a moon's drawn place: `moonAt(b, j, kf)` (with `moonDisp`) in OrbitView::draw. The moons' loop (orbit ring, disc, hit, label) and the follow block both call it: ELP2000-82B Moon, the 13 fitted moons (satState) and the Kepler ellipse, Simple and Real, any year, 2D and 3D, exaggerated distance or True scale. No second copy of the formula.
- Frame: the planet's perspective scale (3D) depends on the camera, which depends on the moon. For the followed moon's planet it is taken as 1 in both places (`moonKf`), so the two cannot disagree (the drawn orbit differs from before by the planet's tiny depth factor only, and only while a moon is followed).
- fly() unchanged (selection and follow; easyZoom keeps the planet's rule). Body, Bary, Far follow unchanged. Journey, Learn's moons stop (learn.cpp) and Alpha Centauri's own follow (alphacen_view.inc) do not use this block's Moon path.
- "Slow time to watch moons" (slowMoons) with a moon selected: speed from that moon's period as before, then the PLANET is selected and followed (the moons circle it). Doc comment in orbit_actions.h; the card link has no tooltip.
- State line: `moonoff=x,y planetoff=x,y` (where the selected moon and its planet were drawn last frame, points from the held middle; 9999 = no moon). check_shell_state drops the two new keys before comparing with the old panel's saved run (on purpose, reason in the test); it passes (121 checks).
**Tests**
- New part check_022.py followmoon (in ui_orbit_view; 1280x800, dark): Earth's Moon 2D and 3D, Io 2D and 3D, lab running at its start speed (7 days a second) for ~60 frames: the moon within 2 points of the middle, its planet moves round it (more than 10 points); slow moons with Io selected gives the planet, followed. 14 checks pass.
- Failing before: with the old follow rule put back (one-line switch, then restored) the same part fails 8 of 14 (the moon 163-285 points from the middle in all four cases, the planet fixed at 0).
- check.sh quick (18 of 18), ui_orbit_view, ui_orbit, ui_shell passed (ui_shell first failed in check_shell_state on the new state keys: fixed as above, that check re-run by hand: 121 checks, 0 failed; ui_shell not re-run in full). No benchmark scene (nothing new is drawn).
