## Causes found
- **Search "stuck" result:** (1) an empty box listed the *recent choices* as results, so the thing just chosen came back after the text was deleted. Now an empty box lists nothing (recent choices only rank equal matches higher). (2) **Ctrl+K with the box already open** cleared our text but Dear ImGui kept its own copy for the same text-box id: old words came back in front of the new ones ("moopluto"). A new search now gets a new text-box id. Not found: any stuck *Moon fact card* (a chosen fact opens the normal Facts panel, which closes on click-away / Esc; checked by pixels).
- **Scrollbar on small dropdowns:** `endPanel` read the window padding AFTER `beginPanel` had popped it (8 px, not the panel's 16 px), so every panel reported a height 16 px too small and had a 16 px scroll range (a scrollbar on a list that fitted; cards clipped by 16 px). Fixed at the source.
- **Legend:** only its arrow closed it; at 880x560 with the Controls panel open the subject card lies over the Legend and hides the arrow (not fixed, see below).
- **See-through rectangle in the toolbar:** the top-light gradient of every pill was a rectangle with square ends reaching 10 px beyond the rounded ends. Pills now keep it inside the straight part.
- **shell_pictures About 1280x800 (item 6): NOT reproduced.** At the pristine HEAD 2cfe740 (separate build) all 8 About pictures, also with `LP_NUM_THREADS=1` and a Mesa cache, matched their saved hashes; the render is the same at frames 40, 41 and 80. So the saved hashes are not stale and nothing timing-dependent was found here; the cause on the fresh machine is unknown (a different machine / font / Mesa is the guess, unproven). Nothing was rewritten for that reason.
