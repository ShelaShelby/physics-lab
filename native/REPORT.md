## What changed (New UI On, both labs)
- **Controls panel** (owner's decision of 6 Oct 2026, in DECISIONS.md and DESIGN.md section 11): one docked panel on the right, 22 em of Body (330 px at 100 %), from under the top bar to 12 u above the bottom, scrolls inside, solid glass, sections that fold (kept in the settings like the cards' state; the panel's own open / closed state too). It is an occluder: the centre shift, the fact line, dropdowns, the subject card (left of it), the Old panel (left of it), the toolbar (centred in the part left of it), the readout and the axis gizmo all sit left of it. Hidden while Learn / Achievements cover the lab.
- **Orbit Lab:** sections Time speed, Show, Pick a body (starts folded), Zoom / sizes / trails, View options: same controls, ids, words and tooltips as the panels they replace. Toolbar: 2D | 3D, **Reset view** (new one-click button), Controls; no carets. Search (Ctrl+K) still opens each one (opens the panel, unfolds the section, scrolls to it).
- **Atom Lab:** a View section: Nucleus / Nucleon, Build another, Reshuffle (same rules as before), Cloud, Cloud 3D, Slice. Toolbar: Atom · Nucleus · Cloud · Chart · Reset · Centre · Drag · Controls, no carets. The rest of the Atom controls stay in the Old panel (5d / 5e). The picture's long text lines wrap before the panel.
- **Decisions I made** (DECISIONS.md, owner may overrule): the Time speed toolbar panel became a section too (the brief: nothing with a caret stays); Pick starts folded; a ring label under the panel is skipped. Segmented buttons whose words do not fit equal parts now size parts to their words ("Lines and names" in 22 em); equal parts are unchanged where they fit.
- **Bug fix A (ring labels):** `orbit/label_clip.h`: a label is drawn only if its whole rectangle is clear of the top bar band (bar + 4), the bottom band and the panel; the rings are still drawn. At 880 x 560 all labels may be skipped (reported).
- **Bug fix B (readout):** Body size (15 px at 100 % Normal; 14 px floor) on a rounded pill, black at 0.6. Finding: **no theme's muted colour reaches 4.5:1 on that pill over a white picture** (best 4.39, Y2K Night), so the text is the theme's text colour, or white where that fails (light themes, Aero, Y2K Ice, Monochrome).
  Measured from the real pictures (python, own WCAG formula; pill and brightest text pixel): space behind: Dark 17.9, Black 17.5, Light / Aero / Paper 20.7; plain light sky (Sky Off) behind: Dark 17.3, Black 17.8, Light 6.0, Aero 8.1, Paper 6.5. Worst case (white behind the pill, all 14 themes, unit test): 4.65 (Solar) to 5.74 (white text).
