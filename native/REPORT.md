# Native Physics Lab 0.10.2 so far (stage 1 of 3: phase colours) — report

Atom Lab only. **The version number is still 0.10.1** (stage 3 changes it); README, ROADMAP, RESUME and the 🕰 history are not updated yet. Older reports are in git history. `index.html` was not touched.

## What changed
- **Complex orbitals (core, `atom/atom_core.*`).** States m = ±1 … ±l for p, d, f (20 states for n ≤ 4), Condon-Shortley phase, made from the existing real angular parts: p(±1) = ∓/± (x ± i y)/√2; d: (xz, yz) and (x²−y², 2xy); f: (xz², yz²), (z(x²−y²), 2xyz), (x(x²−3y²), y(3x²−y²)); +m has the sign (−1)^m. **The 30 real orbitals, their indices and order are untouched**: a complex state has its own id (≥ 1000, `complexState(shell, m)`), shares its shell's radial part, and `psiComplex` gives re and im. Same unnormalised scale as `psi()` (only ratios and |ψ|² are used). `density(id, …)` = |ψ|² for either kind.
- **Setting "Phase colours: Sign / Wheel"** (default Sign, saved as `phase`, Defaults resets it; Cloud, Cloud 3D and Slice, Classic and Glow). Wheel colours each dot by arg(ψ) with a **cyclic map: a circle of constant lightness in CIE L\*a\*b\*** (`atom/phase_wheel.h`: dark pages L\* 72, C 52; light pages L\* 52, C 58; clipped to sRGB; 24 colour bins for drawing). Each dot now stores its phase (`AtomState::ph`; 0 or π for a real orbital). Complex orientations always use the wheel. A colour ring (0, π/2, π, 3π/2, "phase of ψ") is drawn top right when the wheel is on (first put bottom left: it sat under the ⓘ button).
- **Orientation choices** of p, d, f got "m = +1", "m = −1" … (labelled so; default unchanged). The cloud is sampled from |ψ|² with the same steps and random numbers; the 90 % outline works for complex states (it takes ids). The status line says e.g. "4f (m = +3) orbital".
- **Photon jumps from a complex state are not offered:** the dipole rule depends on the light's polarisation, which the lab does not model; Emit/Absorb says so (message). Orbit view is unchanged.
- **App text** (panel note under Phase colours): what phase is, the colour is the phase and not a place or likelihood, m states are complex waves with L_z = mħ and always use the wheel, one electron, non-relativistic, no fine structure (all l of one n share one energy). With the wheel on, the info line and the Slice hint no longer say "two colours = + and −" (the default texts are word for word as before).
- **About, "Where the facts come from":** NIST DLMF section 14.30 (Y_lm and its phase; **page not opened here**, the check was done with SciPy), SciPy and scikit-image (tests only), CIE L\*a\*b\* and IEC 61966-2-1 (formulas written from knowledge, **not looked up**). The About reference changed on purpose: one more native edit (7 to 8, count updated).

## Tests (full suite once: `orbit_tests` 1,377, `atom_tests` 17,871 (+53), `app_tests` 1,367 (+10), `nbody_tests` 36; all pass)
- **a. Winding:** 60 circles round z, all 20 states: total phase change − 2πm ≤ 2.1e-14 rad (limit 1e-9, rounding).
- **b. L_z:** 240 points: |(−i ∂φ)ψ − mψ|/|ψ| ≤ 5.9e-10 (limit 1e-7; the central difference's own error is d²m³/6 = 1.7e-10).
- **c. Orthonormal** (Gauss-Legendre × midpoint × uniform quadrature, 450 × 24 × 24 points): |⟨a|a⟩/norm − 1| ≤ 2.2e-16, complex-complex overlaps ≤ 3.5e-16, with real orbitals of another l ≤ 2.5e-16, and each complex state lies in the span of its shell's real orbitals (sum of squared overlaps = 1 to 3.5e-14). Limit 1e-3 as asked. (Complex and real orbitals of the same l are NOT orthogonal: p(+1) is a mix of px and py.)
- **d. Y_lm against SciPy 1.17.1** (`scipy.special.sph_harm_y`, pip worked; 68 values, table in `tests/reference/ylm_reference.inc` with its script): ours = positive constant × r^l × Y_lm; constant to 8.9e-16, phase off by ≤ 8.9e-16 rad (limit 1e-12).
- **e. Density:** |ψ(±1)|² = (|ψx|²+|ψy|²)/2 for 2p, 3p, 4p to 6.4e-16, and the same with the real pair for d and f (5.3e-16).
- **f. Real dots unchanged:** FNV hash of the 4,800 dots' positions and signs (300 steps, seed 12345) for 6 real setups, **recorded from the 0.10.1 code before the change**, equal now; the 17,818 existing atom checks (whole runs against the web's) also pass. Real dots have phase 0/π matching their sign.
- **g. app_tests:** default Sign, bad values refused (`validChoice`), settings-file round trip (`phase`, `pAx=m-1`), Defaults, the wheel switching on for complex orientations.
- **Also:** wheel colours against scikit-image 0.26.0 `lab2rgb` to 1.4e-4 (limit 2e-3: matrix digits); cyclic; complex 2p dots carry arg(ψ) (1.6e-7 rad; limit 1e-4, float positions); complex 2p outline holds 0.900 of the probability, norm equals the by-hand integral.
- **Limits that were guesses:** the 2p cloud shape (⟨x²⟩ = ⟨y²⟩ within 15 %, ⟨z²⟩ < 0.7⟨x²⟩; measured 10 % and 0.51 against an exact 1:1:0.5, Metropolis dots are correlated), the 0.88 to 0.92 outline mass, the "> 0.25 apart" colours (measured 0.40). Two of my own test numbers were wrong at first (20 states, not 30; point count) and were corrected, not loosened.
