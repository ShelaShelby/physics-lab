# Native Physics Lab 0.10.1 (alpha) — report

General relativity for the Real planets, the Sun's wobble, and two fixes. Older reports are in git history. `index.html` was not touched.

## What changed
- **Stage 1: General relativity (1PN)** (`orbit/nbody.*`, tick box in the Planet physics panel, only shown in Real). Method: the potential of Nobili & Roxburgh 1986, `Phi = -3 (GM_sun)^2 / (c^2 r^2)` of the Sun on each planet, as in REBOUNDx's `gr_potential` (Tamayo et al. 2020, MNRAS 491, 2885). **Source reached:** the formula was read in REBOUNDx's own file `gr_potential.c` on GitHub (a = -6 (GM)^2 d / (c^2 r^4), d = planet minus Sun) and its comment ("gets the precession right, but the mean motion wrong by O(GM/ac^2)"). The 1986 paper, the REBOUNDx paper (arXiv blocked) and Will's review were **not** opened. Why this one: it depends only on position, so it goes into the planet-planet kick of the Wisdom-Holman step and the map stays symplectic and time-reversible; the full Einstein-Infeld-Hoffmann equations are velocity-dependent and would need a different integrator. **Left out (also said in the app):** the relativistic pull of the planets on each other and on the Sun, velocity-dependent terms, the Sun's spin and shape, higher orders; planets' positions along the orbit are off at the order GM/(a c^2) (2.5e-8 for Mercury; I did not measure the coefficient). Default **ON** when Real is on (my choice; the owner can change `ViewSettings::relativity`). Saved as `gr=1|0`; hook `PL_GR`. With it off, results equal 0.10 bit for bit here.
- **Stage 2: the Sun's wobble** (`SolarSystem::barycentre()`, `sunTrail`, `orbit_view.cpp`). **Found:** the picture was already drawn round the barycentre (the web's formula with rough masses), so the 0.10.0 note "the picture stays centred on the Sun, the wobble is not drawn" (and the same sentence in the 0.10.0 🕰 entry, left as history) was **wrong**; the note is corrected. Now, in Real only: the barycentre is the integrator's (minus the Sun's barycentric position, real GMs), the Sun's own path is a fading trail of its positions from the barycentre (a point every 0.05 Sun radii, up to 1,000), and "Exaggerate the Sun's wobble (x50)" (default ON, my choice; `kSunWobbleScale`) draws the path 50 times too big round the barycentre marker, with a ring for the Sun's place on it and the label "Sun's path x50: exaggerated, not to scale". Simple is unchanged (tested). Saved as `wobble`.
- **Stage 3: Milky Way band** (`orbit/sky.*`, `orbit_view.cpp`). **Cause:** the neighbour test in the band code read the `ok` flags of points the same loop had just switched off, so after the first gap in the visible stretch (where the great circle leaves the 100-degree map, or at the first sample) every later point was dropped; the `len > 0.35 diag` rule and the edge fade were innocent. Reproduced without graphics first (scratch copy: only 12 of 137 visible points kept looking straight down). Fix: the neighbour test uses the on-map flags, one-sided directions at the ends, wrap-round at the seam. Position and direction unchanged (tested). The band geometry now lives in `sky.cpp` (`skyScreen`, `milkyWayBand`) so it can be tested. Hook `PL_TILT=yaw,pitch`.
- **Stage 4: secret theme names.** `themeButton` used `"???"` for every secret theme in the width and the wrapped names. New `app::themeShownName(theme, unlocked)` ("???" only while secret and locked) is used for both.
- Version 0.10.1, 🕰 entry, README, ROADMAP, RESUME, OWNER-TODO. About: added REBOUNDx `gr_potential.c`, Nobili & Roxburgh 1986 (not read), Will 2014 (search excerpt only). No new source for the wobble (JPL GMs already listed).

## Tests (full suite once before the commit: `orbit_tests` 1,377, `atom_tests` 17,818, `app_tests` 1,357, `nbody_tests` 36; all pass)
- **GR formula** 6 pi GM / (c^2 a (1-e^2)) (standard result; **not read from a source here**, but it reproduces the published number below): GR on minus off over 500 orbits, Mercury's elements: relative difference -1.3e-5 (step 0.25 d and 1 d alike); a = 0.5 AU, e = 0.5: -9e-6. Limit 1e-3 (fit sees a short-period wobble of ~1e-7 rad against ~2.5e-4 rad). **My first limit for the 1-day step was a guess (2 %); measured 1e-5, so I set it to the same 1e-3.**
- **Published value:** Mercury 42.98 arcsec/century (Will 2014, Living Reviews 17, 4; **from a search excerpt, not the paper**): measured 42.978; eight-planet run, GR on minus off: 42.80 (0.4 % low; limit 2 %, fit noise).
- GR off equals 0.10: bit-identical to an explicit off, and equal (0.0 difference) to the 0.10.0 positions saved before the change (compared to 1e-9 for other compilers).
- GR on, 1,000 years: |dE/E| 2.56e-9 (E includes the GR potential; limit 1e-8, as without GR), |dL|/|L| 8.0e-14 (1e-12), momentum 3.4e-14 (1e-13), centre of mass 0.7 mm (1 km). Time reversal, 100 years: back to 1.5e-10 (1e-9).
- **Wobble:** Sun + Jupiter: Sun's distance = GM_J/(GM_S+GM_J) times the separation to 5.8e-13 at every step (limit 1e-9), min/max distances as ratio x a(1-+e) to 1e-6. Through the clock, 1800-2100, 814 times: centre of mass 1.5 mm (limit 1 m), momentum 3.7e-13 (limit **1e-12, a guess near rounding level**). Eight planets: the Sun is 0.06 to **2.09** Sun radii from the barycentre (largest near 1983); sanity bound from the planets' far points 2.26. **Published check left out:** the only value found (2.098 on 1983-03-15) is from a hobby calculator-program web page, not a paper or JPL; I did not use it as a test. App tests: Real barycentre = minus the Sun's position from the clock (1 mm) and = sum(GM_i x_i)/sum(GM); trail spacing, size, reset on jump; Simple barycentre unchanged.
- **Band:** 624 views (24 turns x 13 tilts x 2 picture shapes): every on-map sample with a neighbour is drawn and no other, one connected run, same great circle, fade changes at most arc/0.2 per step, ends nearly faded out (0.125 of 0.262 allowed), step length at most 1.77 x arc x K (limit 1.8, from the projection). The old logic put back temporarily failed 4 checks (604 of 624 views almost empty); restored.
