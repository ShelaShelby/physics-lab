## Vulkan 1.2 (step 2; rule fixed before looking: add it only if a card family from 2016 or later gains)
- vulkan.gpuinfo.org, Windows, 21 Nov 2026 (sources repo data/gpuinfo/, SOURCES.md): of 1,214 devices, 304 top out at 1.2; 19 of them list both extensions. Families: AMD Polaris (Radeon Pro 555/555X/560/580, 2016-17), Vega (Pro Vega 20/56/64/64X, Vega II, 2017-18), Navi (Pro 5300, W5700X, 2019-20), all on Apple's Boot Camp driver 2.0.204 (report 22394: dynamicRendering and synchronization2 true); the rest are Intel HD 2012-15 and the Direct3D12 translation layer. 2016+ families gain: 1.2 added.
- vk_backend: a 1.2 card is used when both extensions are listed and their feature structures say true; they are enabled only then, and their KHR functions are put in the core names' places (vkCmdBeginRenderingKHR ... vkQueueSubmit2KHR, checked non-null). Settings, the log and the message name it. Tested with PL_VK_PRETEND_API=1.2 on lavapipe: the Black Hole Lab drawn with the synchronisation checks on, zero validation errors; PL_VK_PRETEND_NOEXT (new test switch): the message names the missing extension.
