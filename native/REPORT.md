## Stage 2: quantum beats (`atom/atom_core.*`, `atom_sim.*`, `atom_view.cpp`, `ui/atom_panel.cpp`)
- **Physics.** ψ(t) = a·ψA·e^(−iEA t/ħ) + b·ψB·e^(−iEB t/ħ), both states normalised (`normFactor`), a² = 1 − mix, b² = mix. Energies E_n = −rydM(Z, N) Z²/n² (`levelEV`, reuses `rydM`; h = the lab's own hc ÷ exact c, `kPlanckEVs`). A or B may be a stage-1 complex state. `psiSuper` is given **relative to state A** (multiplied by e^(+iEA t/ħ)), so colours turn once per beat, not at the energy frequency; the app text says so. Same n → ΔE exactly 0 → no beats (density constant); the text says fine structure and the Lamb shift are not modelled.
- **Settings** (Electron card, cloud views only): "Superposition Off / On", "Second orbital" + orientation (same choices, incl. m states), "Mix" 0 to 100 % (default 50), Playback speed shown in cloud views while it is on. Saved (`super`, `orb2`, `pAx2`, `dAx2`, `fAx2`, `mix`); Defaults resets; **benchmark scenes set it Off**; in Orbit view the row is disabled with "A Bohr orbit has no superposition; switch to a cloud view." The wheel is used automatically. Beat clock: 4 s per beat at speed 5, scaling with the speed (`step()` runs only while not paused, so Space pauses it). No photon jumps and no 90 % outline while a superposition is shown (the lab says so).
- **Readout** under the picture: the kind of motion (opposite parity: centre sloshes along z, or moves across z; same parity: breathes; no dipole: only the shape changes; same n: no beats) from a numeric dipole check (`dipoleAxes`), and "real beat period 406 as, slowed by 9.9×10^15" worked out from the numbers.
- **MAIN RISK, measured.** The lab makes **16 dots per physics step = 3,840 dots/s** (Normal), 7,680 (High), 15,360 (Ultra), but the ring grows with the density, so it remembers **3.12 s at every setting**: 78 % of a 4 s beat, not under 10 %. Shorter memory would leave 1,500 dots; so I did neither of the two options. **Chosen:** the dots stay samples of the time-independent mixture q = (|A|²+|B|²)/2 (Metropolis, same steps), and each dot is shown with probability w/2, w = |ψ(x,θ)|²/q ≤ 2 (Cauchy-Schwarz), from a fixed per-dot random number. The shown dots are exact samples of the CURRENT density, no dot remembers the past, and no renderer change was needed (a "not shown" colour group and per-frame phase). Each dot stores its two normalised amplitudes (4 floats) when made; per frame only arithmetic and one atan2 per dot. **Cost here** (software, one core, 24,000 dots): **1.0 to 1.35 ms per frame** for thinning and colouring; about 49 to 50 % of the dots are shown. The drawing itself, the GPU and Windows are not measured. Colours are 24 phase steps (±0.13 rad) on screen; the exact phase is `superPhase`. Still orbitals keep their code path (tests unchanged).

## Tests (full suite once: `orbit_tests` 1,377, `atom_tests` 17,889 (+18), `app_tests` 1,383 (+16), `nbody_tests` 36; all pass)
- **a. Period:** from the zero of ⟨z⟩(t) for 1s + 2p(m=0), a = b: 4.055086856e-16 s against h/(E2−E1) with CODATA 2018 h, Rydberg energy and proton-electron mass ratio **written in the test from knowledge, not looked up**: difference 2.7e-10 (limit 1e-6). Z = 2: ratio 0.249898 of Z = 1 (limit 1e-3: reduced mass differs by 4.1e-4).
- **b. Amplitude:** my own numerical integral of ⟨1s|z|2p0⟩ with textbook R10, R21, Y00, Y10: 0.744936 a0 = 128√2/243 (Fitzpatrick's closed form; **page not opened here**); the lab's ⟨z⟩(0) = 0.744938 = 2ab M to 3e-6 (limit 1e-4); also right for mix 20 %.
- **c. Norm** of 2p(m=+1) + 3d(m=+2) (mix 30 %) at 4 times: |∫|ψ|² − 1| ≤ 2.2e-6 (limit 1e-3). **d.** mix 0 % and 100 %: density exactly time independent; ⟨z⟩ constant. **e.** 2s + 2p: ΔE = 0, density identical at two times, beat period infinite.
- **g. Dots (seeded).** 170 full rings of 1s + 2p, every 400th dot with its own slot (so its own random number): 5,100 dots, ~2,500 shown at each of 4 times. Mean z against 2ab M cos θ: −1.2, −1.2, −1.7, −1.7 standard errors (limit 4); radial chi-square (20 bins, 19 dof) 20.1 to 23.3 (limit 48, the ~99.99 % point). **My first versions of this test were wrong and I fixed them, not loosened them:** the chain is strongly correlated (z autocorrelation 0.98 at lag 1, 0.75 at 16), so stride 64 gave a biased result (4.7 σ off); and reading the same 30 slots in every lab shared 30 random numbers, which breaks the exact thinning. Now: stride 400 (measured autocorrelation −0.004, limit 0.05) and different start slots.
- **h. Colour phase** vs the exact arg(ψ(x, t)) relative to A from the primitives: 1,440 dot-times, ≤ 1.9e-6 rad (limit 1e-4).
- **i. app_tests:** defaults, bad values, settings-file round trip, Defaults, all 5 benchmark scenes Off, beat clock exactly 2π after 4 s at speed 5 and 4π after 2 more s at speed 10, no jumps, same-n and parity texts, Orbit view.
- **Guessed limits:** the dipole threshold 1e-3 (the 1,500-point sum is exact to ~1e-4), the lag-400 limit 0.05. About reference: one more native edit (8 to 9).
