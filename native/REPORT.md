## A. The Orbit Lab's lines (render/lines_card.h, vk_lines.cpp, shaders.h kLinesCS/kLinesVS; orbit/orbit_lines.h, lines_check.h; guard "[vulkan] [lines]")
- The trails, the planets' and dwarf planets' orbits and the distance rings: points kept on the card as a high and a low float of the processor's double (a trail is a ring: only the points it gained are sent; an orbit's 257 corners and a ring's 97 only when they changed). The card projects each piece about a reference point at the view's middle (orbit_view's proj, turn and mild perspective), makes the long pieces near the picture into the processor's curves (a trail's Catmull-Rom, an orbit's ellipse, min(4096, ceil(L/4)) steps; step 0 counts, step 1 one workgroup's prefix sum gives an indirect draw), clips and widens each step as Renderer2D::line in the vertex shader. The processor's LinePen code moved unchanged to orbit/orbit_lines.cpp: the fallback, OpenGL, and the check's reference.
- Check (limits derived first, lines_check.h): 4 orbits, their trails, 6 rings at the heavy view's camera, the closest zoom in 2D and a 3D close-up: every run the processor's number of points; largest difference 0.29, 0.13, 0.54 of its limit. - Pictures, card against processor (PL_NO_LINES_CARD, no MSAA): orbit.heaviest 4, orbit.real_fast 3, orbit.earth 0 pixels over 8 levels of 1,024,000 (check_orbit_ui cardlines, new: 6/0).

## B. The Black Hole Lab's stars: not moved
- The card's sky per pixel has no inverse map. Each image needs the processor's spin table (spin 0: its LensTable) and the table's triangles binned on the sky (90 x 180 bins), and its Newton steps take finite differences at h = 1e-7 rad, under a float's spacing at psi ~ 1 (1.2e-7). A card version needs the tables sent with each new one, analytic derivatives of the table's cubic (edge, tau, the s-stencil) at both spins, the dot rule (starDraw, the true exposure) and its own limits: much bigger than this part. Nothing smaller fits (the 7.0 ms is the inversion). ROADMAP rewritten with this.
## C. The Molecule Lab's Density surface (render/surface_card.h, vk_surface.cpp, kSurfaceCS/kSurfaceVS; molecule/surface_draw.h, surface_check.h; guard "[vulkan] [surface]")
- The surface's vertices and triangles sent once per surface (a vibration: once per phase); the card turns and colours the vertices (molecule_view's light and potential colours), keys each triangle by the float bits of its depth with its number and sorts them (bitonic: by key, then number, so equal depths keep the processor's order), and draws them in that order, each triangle's opacity by its winding. Steps run only when the view or the surface changed.
- Check (limits derived first): benzene 7,066 vertices / 14,128 triangles, water 2,470 / 4,936, water vibrating 2,500 / 4,996: places within 6.1e-5 point (limits 2.5-2.8e-3), colours 3.6e-7 (1e-4), no fall of the processor's depth along the card's order. Pictures (molecule.density, molecule.vib_density, no MSAA): 0 pixels over 8 levels (check_molecule cardsurface, new: 5/0).
- Processor ms: molecule.density 4.34/4.44 -> 4.85/4.67 (noise: the surface's part of the view 1.0 -> 0.002 ms, measured in the view; lavapipe's sort runs on the same 4 cores); molecule.vib_density 10.1/9.2 -> 9.4/7.1.
