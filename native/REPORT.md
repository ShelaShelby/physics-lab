## Part A: rocks (physics: Kepler ellipses from elements; no planet pulls; the Oort cloud is still)
- **Same physics on the GPU:** `kRocksCS` ports `orbit::beltPoint`, `solveKepler` and the camera (`orbit::beltScreen`, one function now shared by the CPU path), each commented with its twin. **Precision rule (DECISIONS):** the time goes in as two floats (exact), `M = M0 + n t` and its reduction to −π..π are in **double** on the GPU (n made on the CPU in double), the Trojans' sway phase is reduced in double per frame; only a small angle is float. Kepler: ≥3 steps, stop below 5e-7, at most 20 (CPU: stop below 1e-9, at most 60, bisection net).
- **Finding (reported, not hidden):** the belt code solved Kepler's equation with exactly 3 Newton steps. Exact for every app rock (largest e 0.498) but 5.8e-3 rad off at e = 0.9, 0.45 rad at 0.99, runaway at 0.999. Replaced by a converged `solveKepler` (same first 3 steps below e = 0.8, so the app's rocks move < 1e-9 of a; the web-reference test at 1e-6 still passes).
- `BeltLayer` (no ImGui) draws CPU or GPU; rocks sent once per set (serial), slots freed when the belts are gone; renderer draws from the compute buffer (no CPU wait, no read-back). Switch **GPU rocks** (Performance card, not saved), report line "Orbit Lab rocks: OpenGL 4.3: GPU rocks on", benchmark scenes **31, 32** = 1, 6 with it Off.
- **Tests (physics written first, `test_rocks.inc`, textbook = the JPL page already in About, read 5 Oct 2026; Kepler by bisection):** solver to bisection ≤1.5e-15 rad for e up to 0.999; `beltPoint` against the textbook, e = 0, 0.2, 0.9, 0.99 and an Oort-like orbit, times to 1e7 years, ≤5e-16 of a; all 3,000 moving app rocks 1e-15; old 3-step solver reproduced and equal to 1e-9. With the old solver put back the file fails 22,564 checks. **GPU (`gl_rocks.inc`, written after the shader's first draft, before it first ran):** every rock of scenes 1 and 6 at 0, 0.25, 1, 100, 1e4, 1e5, 1e6, 1e7 years and after 200 real seconds at the fastest speed (10,066 years), three cameras (3D ×1, top-down ×30, following ×10): **worst 0.0025 point** over 404,000 rocks near the picture (stated bound 0.01); farther out ≤2.3e-4 of the coordinate (float cancellation near the perspective limit, bound 3e-4); against the textbook 5e-7 of a (6 orbits × 40 rocks × 8 times; bound 2e-7+4e-7/(1−e)). Pictures of both scenes, selected belt too: 0–3 of 741,520 pixels differ. Logic: 4 uploads for 50 frames, none for the CPU path, new belts re-sent, a stale CPU cache caught, 17 sets fall back to the CPU, a failing or absent compute object falls back, forced 3.3: CPU path, picture identical.

## Part B: the chart kept on the GPU
- **What is cached:** flat: bands, 3,557 tiles, edges; landscape: ground + blocks (tops, sides, outlines, marks), in world coordinates (`chart_mesh.*`, `render/mesh.h`); `kChartVS` projects with uniforms (tilt, zoom, pan: no rebuild); hairlines are two end points pushed half a point on the screen. **Rebuild triggers (all):** picture size, theme colours, height meaning (landscape). Not: tilt, zoom, pan, chosen/hovered nuclide (drawn per frame), legend, chain. Switch **GPU chart cache**; scenes **33, 34** = 19, 20 with it Off. OpenGL 3.3 is enough: no fallback needed, tested on a forced 3.3 context.
- **Tests (`test_chart_cache.inc`, `gl_chart.inc`; written after the builders, before first run):** the mesh equals the per-frame builders' output (409,004 triangles; same count, order, colours; flat 3 sizes × 2 palettes; landscape 9 views × 2 palettes incl. both heights, tilt 1/0.5/0.02/0, zoom up to 16, pan) with positions within 1e-3 pt (worst 0.00098, a stated float-rounding bound); each trigger rebuilds and nothing else does; draw order = the painter's order; **hit test:** the topmost block the mesh draws under a grid of pointers is the one `pick()` answers (hollow squares are pickable, drawn as outline only: handled); pictures of scenes 19–22, tilts, zoom/pan, light/dark: 0–7 of 741,520 pixels differ; the second cached frame sends nothing.
- **Perturbed and caught:** 10 for A (GLSL Kepler stopped at 2 steps, time in float, M reduced in float, camera sign, sway phase dropped, re-upload every call, CPU cache blind to new belts, libPhase unreduced, `keepOnly` missing, old solver) and 10 for B (blocks near-to-far, height / theme / size trigger missing, rebuild every frame, side-face colours swapped, CPU twin keeps culled faces, shader tilt sign, shader line side, shader side-face rule). **Two slipped through first:** without `keepOnly` the test crashed instead of failing (the logic test also exposed a real flaw: the slot table cleared itself mid-frame; fixed to free only dead sets); the shader's side-face rule changes 1 pixel in the real pictures (equivalent to the eye), so `chartShaderRules` tests it on a purpose-made mesh (now caught). All reverted, hashes identical.
