## C. The Black Hole Lab (speed only)
- extreme, all_light: their frame thread's extra was the panel's shadow size (132 edges by bisection whenever spin, distance or tilt moved: shell 2.6-4.9 -> 1.1-1.2 ms here). - film_tilt: nothing holds it. It rests 40 % of its counted time, and its new pictures were divided by the whole time; in the owner's run (2,640 frames, 738 pictures, GPU 1.26 ms a frame) every moving frame got a new picture, at about 4 ms of card time each. The benchmark now counts new pictures a second over the time the view moved (app_tests: new check). Expect film_tilt about 237 a second: the sky pass itself limits it (ROADMAP).

The 7.2 ms acquire: the benchmark runs with VSync off (IMMEDIATE) and 2 swapchain images, so the next image is free only once the card has finished the frame before: it is the card's time on a new sky picture, not the processor's.
## D. The crash-safe helper (app/gpu_helper.*, render/vk_helper.cpp)
- At a Vulkan start, each guarded program not yet passed with this driver that the helper knows (light paths, Classic dots, nucleus density) is made first by `PhysicsLab --gpu-helper` (no window; one process each, in turn); its guard's hold() waits. Passed: the app runs its steps as before; died: kept "off", "the helper died making <step>". Pipelines only (not the first small run). Its cache file is merged into the app's at the next start.
- Detective work: while the light paths are off, once per driver, five variants of kKerrTableCS, one helper each: math functions alone, launch + one step, the edge alone, one whole ray, the whole shader with loops bounded at 64. Here all five made (5-475 ms).
- check_shell gpuhelper (new, ui_shell): 9 of 9. guardturn now sets PL_NO_GPU_HELPER (the app's own step): 4 of 4.
