## Found and fixed or reported
- Cloud density said "48,000 dots" for every electron: the cloud keeps 48,000 per electron at Ultra, at most 240,000 (12,000/60,000, 24,000/120,000); the choices' texts now say so; app_tests checks the counts.
- Oganesson-294 decays in about a millisecond: the Atom scenes hold the decay clock (0.56.0's scenes used uranium). Reduced motion held the jumps and swings: the run sets Motion off and gives it back.
- nucleus_density: the density grid is made once while still (the 0.50.3 suspect is gone), 48 times in 2.6 s turning.
- Here the Black Hole Lab never settled while the cores were full (workers below normal priority): blackhole_spin 6.4 s, molecule_blackhole capped, film_tilt's rest comparison not reached (it says so in the report). Waits run with VSync on for that reason; on his PC this is to be read.
- No scene was left out. atom.all_classic: Phase colours do not apply to the whole atom (its colours are the shells); the text says so.
