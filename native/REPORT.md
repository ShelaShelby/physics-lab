# 0.21 stage 1: Molecule Lab "Realistic" style (Windows, a real GPU and a real mouse NOT tested)

## What changed
- Molecule Lab, Viewer, Style: Ball-and-stick | Space-filling | **Realistic** (panel only; the bottom bar and the two old styles' look are unchanged; Build mode draws Realistic as ball-and-stick). Shows the CALCULATED electron density at the app's own measured shape: a Glow dot cloud (Atom Lab Cloud 3D code, `glowBlobs` moved unchanged into atom/cloud_dots), 20k / 40k / 60k (default) / 100k dots; a translucent density surface at 0.001 / 0.0016 / 0.002 (default) / 0.01 e/bohr³ coloured by the electrostatic potential (red −, white 0, blue +, symmetric per molecule, colour bar in au and kJ/mol per e, 1 au = 2625.50 kJ/mol, CODATA 2022) or plain; Cloud + surface | Cloud | Surface; nuclei as small points (click: atom info); no bonds. Honesty notes in the panel and About (calculated not measured, no hard edge, AFM shows forces (Gross et al. 2009), dots are probabilities, potential at equilibrium, ozone closed-shell approximation, O2 triplet).
- Data: new tool `tools/make_density_data.py` (PySCF 2.14.0, B3LYP = libxc 402, aug-cc-pVTZ for all 15, grid level 3; O2 UKS triplet, others RKS) at exactly the app's coordinates (new `molecule_xyz` exporter -> `data/molecules/xyz/`). Per molecule: density grid 0.25 bohr (uint16 log), fine cubes at non-H nuclei for the integrals (the cusp), potential grid 0.4 bohr (int16), dipole from the density matrix, data sheet marked COMPUTED. 5.5 MB in `data/molecules/density/` (README there), embedded by `cmake/embed_density.cmake` as 64-bit words (cmake ~2 s, compile ~3 s); the Linux exe grows by about 5.7 MB (embedded object 5,695,992 bytes). Slowest molecule: benzene, 10 min (no aug-cc-pVDZ fallback needed).
- Surface: **marching tetrahedra** (each grid cube cut into 6 Kuhn tetrahedra), not the 256-case marching-cubes table: chosen because it can have no cracks (closed surface guaranteed) and needs no long table typed from memory; on ln ρ at 0.5 bohr, normals from the density gradient; cached per molecule + isovalue; triangles sorted far to near only when the view changes. Approximation (painter's order, no depth buffer): the whole surface is drawn over the whole cloud and nuclei; triangles sorted by centroid.
- Settings: `style=real` and ` rdots=60000 riso=0.002 rcol=pot rshow=both` appended to the v1 molecule line only after Realistic was used (a 0.20 line is written back byte for byte); bad fields fall back. Test env `PL_MOLECULE_STYLE=real`, `PL_MOLECULE_REAL`.
- Found by the first Windows run (failed, density_logic: "C6H6 nuclei vs viewer atoms, worst 4.19 A"): the Jacobi placement in molecule.cpp gives compiler-dependent axes when two moments are equal, so MSVC turned benzene in its plane. Root fix (owner's choice): the 15 viewer molecules are now drawn in the stored frame `data/molecules/xyz` (written at full double precision, %.17g), embedded as `src/molecule/viewer_frame.inc` by `tools/make_viewer_frame.py`; no re-diagonalising at start-up. The frame is used only if every distance matches the shape built from the CCCBDB values (1e-8 A), else the placement is kept and Realistic draws nothing. All 15 densities were recomputed at these exact coordinates. New check: every viewer molecule uses its stored frame. Extra pushes: a stray settings_write_test/s.txt (from a hand run of app_tests), then this fix.
- Also fixed (found by molecule_pictures failing on a fast run, alone too): settings are written 0.25 s (wall clock) after the last change, so a change in the app's last quarter second was lost; the app now writes them at once when it closes (main.cpp, settings.cpp). The test failed before and passes after.
- Deleted the stray native/in.txt, o1.txt, o2.txt (grep: nothing referenced them). Version 0.21 (CMake, workflow title), changelog 0.21, About sources (PySCF, B3LYP, aug-cc-pVTZ, libxc, CCCBDB dipoles with codes, Bader 0.002, the 2024 study NOT read (as given), Gross et al.
