## The height mapping (a chosen scale, not a measurement; stated in `chart_view.h`, the legend, About)
**Height = (log10(half-life in s) + 23) / 55**, cut to 0..1: base 10 (each step up is ×10 in half-life), bottom 10⁻²³ s, top 10³² s. The ends are the table's shortest (8.6e-23 s, H-5) and longest (7.1e31 s, Te-128) half-lives rounded outwards to whole powers of ten, so nothing is cut. One scale height is drawn **12 tiles tall**, chosen by looking (36 made the stable ridge hide the whole proton-rich side; 10 and 12 read well). Median half-life sits at 0.44 of the scale.
**The four groups, plus two** (counts = tiles): **stable** 251 (182 plain + 69 where NUBASE2020 gives a limit and/or a "?" entry): one fixed plateau at 1.1, *above* the top, solid, the 69 with a small mark on top (stage 2's distinction; NUBASE's other 2 "stbl", Kr-78 and Xe-124, have a measured half-life in the lab's table and are on the scale). **No measured half-life** 87 (84 blank + 3 "p-unst"): **no height**, flat and hollow (outline only). **Extrapolated** (NUBASE "#") 369: **no height**, flat, pale fill and outline; the number is never used. **Only a limit** 11: a pale block at the limit's height (">4.6 zs", "<10 ps"…; units parsed from the file). **Approximate** 119 (113 of the lab's "~" + 6 NUBASE "~"): pale block at the value. **Measured** 2,720: solid. Total 3,557. No unknown or extrapolated tile has a height (tested over all tiles).

## Projection, order, hit test
Fixed isometric: turn 45°, tip 54.736° from straight down (= arccos(1/√3), camera 35.264° above the horizon), both × tilt; at full tilt the three axes look sqrt(2/3) = 0.8165 of their length (checked by hand). Tilt 0, zoom 1, no pan: the projection is the identity (stage 2's own drawing code and grid lookup are used). Zoom is about the pointer; the pan is 0 at zoom 1 and is cut so ≥ 120×90 pt of the chart stays in the picture; zoom 1..16.
**Order:** blocks are drawn far to near by N + (118 − Z); the viewer is on the high-N, low-Z side, and a block can only be hidden by one with N and (118 − Z) both ≥, so this order is right at every tilt in (0, 1]; it is **fixed (sorted once), nothing is sorted per frame**. **Hit rule:** *the topmost drawn block at the pixel* (last drawn whose top or either visible side face covers it); hover and click use it; a click on a side face chooses that block. Blocks are rebuilt only when the picture size, tilt, zoom or pan changes.
