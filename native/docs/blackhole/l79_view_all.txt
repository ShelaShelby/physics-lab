Made by PL_PIC_L79=1 PL_PIC_ALL=1 build/blackhole_picture --disc: spin 0, the camera at 1000 GM/c^2 (the lab's farthest; Luminet's observer is at infinity),
10 degrees above the disc's plane, 4 degrees across the picture's height, no sky; the feeding preset's disc (6 to 2000 GM/c^2), Light: All light, automatic
exposure. No copy of the paper's figure is in this repository.
Luminet 1979 (J.-P. Luminet, A&A 75, 228), Fig. 11 p. 235, "Simulated photograph of a spherical black hole with thin accretion disk", a "bolometric photography"
made from his Figs 9-10 (the observed bolometric flux F0 = FS/(1+z)^4): a dark shadow with the thin ring of the disc's secondary image hugging it, the disc's
far side bent up over the hole, one side of the disc much brighter than the other next to the shadow, the light fading outward with no visible rim; drawn as
dots on paper. This picture shows the same features; its bright side is on the right because the lab's gas turns the other way round (on the left in L79).
Numbers: L79's Fig. 9 (30 degrees) has its largest curve 1.80 with a small unlabelled curve inside it; this lab's largest is 2.11 F_S max there. Fig. 10
(10 degrees) prints 2.62 as the largest; this lab gives 2.81 (REPORT 0.58.0: not reproduced; the figure itself draws no 2.40 curve).
